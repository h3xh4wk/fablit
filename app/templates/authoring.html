Authoring brief

Describe the practice you want. The seven marked fields are required and grouped into three steps; the optional ones stay out of the way until you need them.

1. What the practice is for

Who it is for and the thinking it is meant to develop.

The subject area, for example visual analysis or writing.

Who the learner is and what they are working on.

The capability the practice is designed to strengthen.

2. How the learner will work

The shape of the practice and the response it asks for.

3. How the work will be reviewed with the learner

What the evaluation looks for, and how that becomes feedback and reflection. Describe qualitative intent: what to notice in the learner's thinking, never a rating of it.

Optional context and constraints — five fields, skip any you do not need
