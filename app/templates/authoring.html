Authoring brief

Describe the practice you want. The seven marked fields are required and grouped into three steps; the optional ones stay out of the way until you need them.

Help me write the brief

Start from rough notes, a pasted source, or just a topic. Fablit drafts the fields below and marks what came from your notes, what is a suggestion, and what needs your confirmation. Partial notes are fine, and you review and edit every field before generating a candidate.

Used only as source material for the draft. Fablit never presents an official examination or institutional requirement as fact.

Drafting the brief from your notes. This can take a moment.
