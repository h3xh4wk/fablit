# Learner Instructions (SPEC-014 §30)

Participants should receive only the public URL and the single sentence below.
No other guidance: no instructions on where to click, what to say, how the
feedback works, or which activity to choose.

> **Open Fablit and explore. Choose any activity that catches your interest,
> complete it, and tell us what you think.**

## How to share

Send each invited learner:

1. The pilot URL (HTTPS).
2. The sentence above.
3. An invitation to share their thoughts afterwards (verbally, in writing, or
   via any channel they prefer — see [feedback-record.md](feedback-record.md)).

## What not to tell learners

- Do not explain the dashboard or the activities.
- Do not suggest which activity to try.
- Do not explain how feedback is generated.
- Do not hint at expected answers or outcomes.

The pilot exists partly to observe whether the interface communicates these
things naturally (§30). If a learner asks *"What am I supposed to do here?"*,
treat the question as evidence first, and record it
([feedback-record.md](feedback-record.md), §31).
